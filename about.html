MY APPROACH

Curious by nature.
Practical by choice.

I’m Emmanuel Amoh, an IT support and infrastructure professional based in Accra, Ghana. My work and interests sit at the intersection of end-user technology, infrastructure operations, cloud computing and cybersecurity.

I enjoy understanding why technology fails, finding clear ways to restore service and helping people use their tools effectively. I’m intentional about moving beyond theory by building small, documented technical projects.

My current development focus includes AWS cloud services, networking fundamentals, security operations and infrastructure troubleshooting.

This portfolio is a working record of my learning and project development. Project statuses are identified honestly so visitors can distinguish completed work from planned labs.
